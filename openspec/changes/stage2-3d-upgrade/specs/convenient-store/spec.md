# convenient-store delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Readable Three.js store
The play scene SHALL show labeled packaging, a glass refrigerator for milk, a checkout, environmental lighting, and shoppers with walk/pick/pay/leave motion. Low-stock SHALL be visible. Restock SHALL be a large contextual control. Camera SHALL keep shelves pickable above the HUD. Hidden tabs SHALL pause the shift. WebGL failure SHALL explain itself without replacing the scene with a static screenshot. Simulation scoring SHALL stay unchanged.
