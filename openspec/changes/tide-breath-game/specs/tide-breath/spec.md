# 潮汐呼吸

## Playable night ocean

- **WHEN** a guest opens 潮汐呼吸 play
- **THEN** a Three.js night sea mounts, hold/release changes the water, a guided creature can collect starlight, and unlocking shows 3D coral
- **AND** progress reloads from `qingxin.games.tide-breath`
- **AND** 云上散步 and 记忆货架 remain 即将到来
